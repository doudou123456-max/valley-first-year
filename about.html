About Valley First Year

Unofficial fan site · Not affiliated with ConcernedApe

Valley First Year is a free, unofficial Stardew Valley guide site focused on Year 1 checklists, calm priorities, and “what to skip” advice for new farmers.

Who operates this site

This is an independent fan project. We publish practical English guides for beginners. We are not a studio, retailer, or official publisher. Contact: see the Contact page.

Editorial approach

  • Guides prioritize decisions and routines over wiki dumps.
  • Drafting tools may help research speed; published pages are human-edited for clarity and accuracy.
  • We prefer priority comparisons over fabricated drop rates or fake “guaranteed” outcomes.
  • We update pages when major game updates change early-game advice.

Independence & trademarks

Fan-made guide site — not affiliated with ConcernedApe. Stardew Valley and related names are trademarks of their respective owners. Valley First Year is not endorsed by or sponsored by the rights holders.

Advertising
