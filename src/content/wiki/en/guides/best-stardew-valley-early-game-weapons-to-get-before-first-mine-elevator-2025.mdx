# Best Stardew Valley Early-Game Weapons to Get Before First Mine Elevator (2025)

If you’ve ever died on mine level 38 after spending 2 hours collecting 50 copper ore for your first tool upgrade, you know just how critical a reliable early-game weapon is in Stardew Valley. The first mine elevator unlocks at level 40, meaning you have to fight your way through 40 floors of slimes, bugs, rock crabs, and shadow brutes without a quick way to restart your run if things go wrong. The right weapon cuts down fight times, reduces damage taken, and lets you clear levels faster so you can hit that 40th floor milestone earlier in your first spring. Below, we break down the best Stardew Valley early game weapons before first mine elevator unlocks, where to find them, and how to upgrade them without relying on random chest drops.

## Why Early-Game Weapons Matter for Mine Progression

Your starting rusty sword, given to you by Marlon when you first enter the mines, only deals 3-8 damage. That’s enough for the first 5 floors of slimes, but by level 15, you’ll be spending 4+ hits to take down a single green slime, and rock crabs will soak up 10+ hits while dealing 6 damage per hit back. Without a better weapon, you’ll burn through foraged food and health restoratives twice as fast, and you’ll rarely make it past level 30 before passing out from exhaustion at 2 a.m.

The first elevator unlocks at level 40, so every run before that requires you to start back at level 1 if you die or leave the mines. A strong early weapon solves this by:
- Reducing fight time per enemy, so you can clear 10+ levels per in-game hour
- Lowering damage taken, meaning you don’t have to waste inventory space on extra food
- Letting you kill flying bugs and rock crabs before they can ambush you in narrow mine corridors
- Making it easier to farm copper and iron ore for tool upgrades, which speed up all other parts of your farm progression

If you’re focused on optimizing your first year, upgrading your weapon before you start serious mine runs is one of the highest-ROI decisions you can make. We recommend getting a better weapon by Spring 10 at the latest, so you can hit level 40 by mid-Spring and unlock the first elevator for faster ore farming later on. For more tips on mine progression in your first season, check out our [Stardew Valley Spring Mine Guide](/guides/spring-mine-progression).

## Top 3 Weapons You Can Get Before Reaching Mine Level 40

All of these weapons are obtainable before you hit mine level 40, and none of them require random loot chest drops or lucky RNG to acquire. They’re ranked by overall utility for mine levels 1-40, with factors like damage output, attack speed, and ease of acquisition taken into account.

### 1. Steel Smallsword
The Steel Smallsword is the most well-rounded stardew valley early game weapon for mine levels 1-40, with a base damage of 12-18 and a fast attack speed that lets you stun-lock most common enemies. It also has a +1 critical hit chance bonus, which means you’ll land one-shot kills on slimes and bugs far more often than you would with slower, higher-damage alternatives.

What makes it the top pick is its accessibility: you can buy it as soon as you have 1000g, no mine progression required. Unlike weapons you find in chests, you can pick it up on Spring 5 if you make quick money from selling parsnips or foraged daffodils and leeks. For players who hate relying on random drops, it’s the most consistent option for clearing the first 40 mine floors quickly.

**Stats Breakdown:**
- Damage: 12-18
- Attack Speed: Fast (+2 speed rating)
- Bonus: +1 critical hit chance
- Ideal for: All players, especially those who want a reliable weapon without grinding for drops

### 2. Insect Head
The Insect Head is a free, surprisingly powerful spear-type weapon that drops from bug enemies in the first 20 mine floors. It deals 10-16 damage, which is slightly lower than the Steel Smallsword, but it has a 3-tile range that lets you hit enemies from far enough away to avoid taking damage entirely. It’s perfect for dealing with flying cave bugs, rock crabs that hide in their shells, and dust sprites that swarm you in groups.

The only catch is that you have to kill enough bugs to get it to drop, but drop rates are relatively high: most players get one within their first 3 runs through levels 1-20. If you don’t want to spend 1000g on the Steel Smallsword, the Insect Head is the best free alternative, and it’s even better than the Steel Smallsword for players who prefer to fight from a safe distance.

**Stats Breakdown:**
- Damage: 10-16
- Attack Speed: Medium
- Bonus: 3-tile long range
- Ideal for: Players who want a free weapon or struggle with getting swarmed by enemies

### 3. Wood Club
If you prefer heavy, high-damage weapons that can kill most common enemies in one hit, the Wood Club is the best early option. It deals 15-25 damage per hit, the highest of any pre-level 40 weapon, and it has a knockback bonus that sends enemies flying backward so they can’t hit you back. The tradeoff is its slow attack speed, which means you have to time your hits carefully to avoid taking damage from fast-moving enemies like dust sprites.

You can buy the Wood Club from the Adventurer’s Guild for 500g, half the price of the Steel Smallsword, so it’s a great budget option for players who are saving gold for seed purchases or farm upgrades. It’s especially good for dealing with shadow brutes and shadow shamans that start appearing on levels 30-40, as most of them will go down in 2 hits or less.

**Stats Breakdown:**
- Damage: 15-25
- Attack Speed: Slow (-1 speed rating)
- Bonus: +2 knockback
- Ideal for: Budget players, or those who prefer heavy, high-damage weapons

## Where to Find Each Early-Game Weapon (No RNG Required)

All of these weapons are guaranteed or near-guaranteed to get if you follow the steps below, no lucky chest drops needed. This is exactly how to get good weapons early in stardew valley without wasting hours grinding for random loot.

### How to Get the Steel Smallsword
The Steel Smallsword is available for purchase from the Adventurer’s Guild, located to the right of the mine entrance, as soon as you unlock the mines (which happens when you receive the letter from the Joja Corporation about the mine landslide on Spring 5).

To get it:
1. Earn 1000g by selling spring crops (parsnips, potatoes), foraged goods (daffodils, leeks, wild horseradish), or fish caught in the river or ocean.
2. Head to the Adventurer’s Guild anytime between 2 p.m. and 10 p.m. (the guild is closed outside of these hours).
3. Talk to Marlon behind the counter, select the “Shop” option, and purchase the Steel Smallsword from his inventory.
4. Equip it immediately to replace your rusty sword.

This is the fastest and most reliable way to get a top-tier early weapon, and we recommend it for all first-time players who want to avoid frustration in the mines.

### How to Get the Insect Head
The Insect Head drops from Cave Bugs, Grubs, and Cave Flies, all of which spawn commonly on mine levels 1-20. Drop rates are approximately 5% per kill, so you’ll usually get one after killing 20-30 bug enemies, which takes one or two short mine runs.

To farm it efficiently:
1. Bring your starting rusty sword and 2-3 foraged food items (like salmonberries or blackberries if it’s the right season, or leeks/daffodils in spring) to heal.
2. Run through mine levels 1-20, killing every bug enemy you see and ignoring slimes and rock crabs if you don’t want to waste time.
3. If you don’t get the drop on your first run, exit the mines, sleep, and try again the next day: enemy spawns reset every day, so you’ll have a fresh batch of bugs to kill.
4. Once it drops, equip it immediately: its long range makes clearing the rest of the first 40 floors much easier.

The Insect Head is also usable as a weapon for the [Spring Flower Dance](/guides/spring-event-guide) mine runs if you get it early enough, letting you get a head start on iron ore farming.

### How to Get the Wood Club
The Wood Club is also sold by Marlon at the Adventurer’s Guild, for only 500g, half the price of the Steel Smallsword. It’s available as soon as you unlock the mines, same as the Steel Smallsword.

To get it:
1. Earn 500g from selling foraged goods, fish, or your first harvest of parsnips. You can usually get this much gold by Spring 6 or 7 if you sell all the foraged items you find on your farm and around Pelican Town.
2. Head to the Adventurer’s Guild between 2 p.m. and 10 p.m., talk to Marlon, and purchase the Wood Club from his shop.
3. Practice using its slow attack speed on low-level slimes before heading into higher mine floors, so you get used to timing your hits to avoid taking damage.

The Wood Club is the best option for players who are saving gold for strawberry seeds at the Spring Egg Festival, as it only costs half as much as the Steel Smallsword and still deals enough damage to clear the first 40 mine floors.

## How to Upgrade Your First Weapon Efficiently

Once you have one of the three weapons above, you can upgrade it at the Blacksmith to make it even more powerful, before you hit mine level 40. Upgrading your weapon will cut down fight times even more, and it will stay useful for the next 40 mine floors (levels 40-80) as well.

### Step 1: Gather the Required Materials
To upgrade a weapon, you need to bring Clint the Blacksmith the weapon, 5 copper bars, 2 iron bars, and 2000g for the first upgrade (copper tier), or 10 iron bars and 5000g for the iron tier upgrade. We recommend going for the copper upgrade first, as it’s much cheaper and still provides a significant damage boost.

To get the required bars:
- Copper ore spawns on mine levels 1-39, with higher spawn rates on levels 10-20. You need 25 copper ore to make 5 copper bars (each bar takes 5 ore and 1 coal to smelt in a furnace).
- Iron ore spawns on mine levels 20-39, with higher spawn rates on levels 30-39. You need 10 iron ore to make 2 iron bars.
- Coal can be found from killing dust sprites (levels 40-79, but some spawn on levels 30-39 too) or from breaking coal rocks in the mines.

You can craft a furnace with 20 copper ore and 25 stone, which you unlock after reaching mining level 4. If you don’t want to smelt bars yourself, you can also buy copper bars from Clint for 600g each, but that’s much more expensive than smelting them yourself. For a full guide to smelting and ore farming, check out our [Stardew Valley Mining Guide for Beginners](/guides/beginner-mining-tips).

### Step 2: Plan Your Upgrade Timing
Upgrading a weapon takes 2 in-game days, so you need to plan around that to avoid wasting time when you could be clearing mine floors. We recommend dropping off your weapon for upgrade on a day when you have farm work to do, like harvesting crops, watering, or completing quests around Pelican Town, so you don’t lose out on mine progress.

A sample upgrade schedule for your first spring:
- Spring 7: Buy the Steel Smallsword or Wood Club from the Adventurer’s Guild
- Spring 8-12: Farm copper and iron ore, smelt bars, and save up 2000g for the upgrade
- Spring 13: Drop off your weapon at Clint’s for the copper upgrade
- Spring 13-14: Work on your farm, fish, or complete town quests while you wait for the upgrade
- Spring 15: Pick up your upgraded weapon, and start clearing mine levels 20-40 to unlock the first elevator

### Step 3: Enchant Your Weapon (Optional, Pre-Level 40)
If you have access to the Sewers (which you unlock by donating 60 items to the Museum, possible as early as late Spring), you can get a weapon enchantment from the Wizard for 10,000g and a prismatic shard, but prismatic shards are extremely rare pre-level 40, so we don’t recommend grinding for them early on. A much better optional upgrade is to apply a “Crusader” or “Vampiric” ring, which you can find in chests on levels 10-30. Crusader rings increase damage against shadow enemies (common on levels 30-40), and Vampiric rings give you a 10% chance to heal 1 health per kill, which reduces the amount of food you need to bring on mine runs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best weapon for mine levels 1-40?
The Steel Smallsword is the best overall weapon for mine levels 1-40, thanks to its fast attack speed, high damage, and reliable availability from the Adventurer’s Guild. If you don’t want to spend 1000g, the Insect Head is the best free alternative, with long range that lets you avoid damage from most enemies.

### Can you get a good weapon before entering the mines?
Yes! You can buy both the Steel Smallsword and the Wood Club from the Adventurer’s Guild as soon as you unlock the mines on Spring 5, before you even step foot inside the mine entrance. All you need is enough gold from selling crops, foraged goods, or fish.

### How do you get a better sword than the rusty sword early?
The fastest way is to buy the Steel Smallsword or Wood Club from the Adventurer’s Guild for 1000g or 500g respectively. If you don’t want to spend gold, you can farm the Insect Head from bug enemies on mine levels 1-20, which usually drops after 20-30 kills.

### Is the Insect Head better than the Steel Smallsword?
It depends on your playstyle. The Insect Head has longer range, which is great for players who prefer to fight from a distance, but it has lower damage and slower attack speed than the Steel Smallsword. The Steel Smallsword is better for players who like to get up close and stun-lock enemies with fast hits.

### Should I upgrade my weapon before hitting level 40?
Yes, upgrading your weapon to copper tier will increase its damage by 30% or more, making it much easier to clear levels 30-40, where tougher shadow enemies start spawning. The upgrade only costs 5 copper bars, 2 iron bars, and 2000g, which is a small investment for the time you’ll save clearing mine floors.

With the right weapon, you’ll unlock the first mine elevator by mid-Spring, letting you farm iron ore at will and upgrade all your tools before summer starts.
